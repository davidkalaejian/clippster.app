## Description
Clippster is a lightweight, native macOS menu bar clipboard manager with
on-device AI superpowers. Everything you copy — text, URLs, images, files —
plus every screenshot you take becomes a searchable record, accessible right
from your menu bar. No cloud, no accounts. Everything stays on your Mac.

**NEVER LOSE A COPY AGAIN**
Every copy is silently saved to a persistent, on-disk history that survives
restarts. Need that link, snippet, or image from three days ago? It's still
there. Pin the clips you need forever — they're safe even when old history
is automatically pruned.

**TWO WAYS TO RECALL, INSTANTLY**
- Menu bar popover: press the global hotkey ⌥⌘V from anywhere — or click the
  menu bar icon — for your latest 15 clips with real, live previews. Navigate
  the whole popover from your keyboard: ↑↓ to move, ↵ to copy a clip and close,
  ⇧↵ to copy the text recognized out of an image or screenshot, Esc to close,
  ⌫ to delete, P to pin or unpin, and ⌘O to jump into the full Manager. Prefer
  the trackpad? Hover any clip for floating action buttons — Copy, Copy scanned
  text, Pin, or Delete.
- Manager window: a full searchable history grouped by day, with Pinned and
  category filter chips. Search by text or by meaning — semantic search finds
  the clip you're thinking of, not just the words you typed.

**SCREENSHOTS, AUTOMATICALLY**
Every screenshot you take is captured as a clip the moment it lands in your
Screenshots folder. The extracted text is searchable and copyable on the spot.

**ON-DEVICE AI THAT RESPECTS YOUR PRIVACY**
Every clip is enriched entirely on your Mac by Apple's frameworks — no data
ever leaves your machine.
- OCR (Vision): text is read out of images and screenshots.
- Auto-categorization: a hybrid classifier sorts your clips — links, file
  paths, code, credentials, and more. Anything that looks like a password
  or API key is flagged with a key icon.
- Embeddings (NaturalLanguage): sentence vectors power semantic search.

**PRIVACY GUARDRAIL**
Content from password managers is never captured in the first place.

**BUILT TO STAY OUT OF THE WAY**
Near-zero idle CPU. No background daemons, no network, no logins. Just a
menu bar icon and a global hotkey. Runs at login if you want it to, stays
silent if you don't.

**EXPORT & RESET**
Export your full history to JSON with one click. Clear everything just as
easily (with a confirmation, of course).

Clippster is native Swift and SwiftUI — built for macOS 14 and later.
Auto-categorization with Foundation Models requires macOS 26 and Apple
Intelligence; everything else works on macOS 14.
